Exploratory setting transitions by origin-episode completion and PSUD

Quasi-independence among setting changes; expected counts fitted separately within each group

The analysis includes 40,512 consecutive admission pairs among 27,445 recurrent-care patients. Groups use PSUD at admission and completion status at discharge from the current episode; the destination is the next observed admission. Off-diagonal cells show observed/expected (O/E), observed counts (O), and expected counts (E). Expected counts preserve each group's origin and destination margins among setting changes under quasi-independence. Gray diagonal cells show same-setting counts, excluded from that model. An asterisk indicates an observed or expected count below 5. Colors saturate at O/E <=0.125 and >=8; labels show actual ratios, including zero. Results are descriptive and unweighted. Patients may contribute dependent pairs to multiple groups; between-group O/E differences are not risk ratios or causal effects. Non-completion includes dropout, referral, and administrative discharge or truncation. GP, general population; WO, women-only; PSUD, polysubstance use disorder; bas, basic ambulatory; int, intensive ambulatory; res, residential.
